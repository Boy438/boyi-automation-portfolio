## Longer bio

I help small teams replace repetitive manual work with small, reliable workflows. The goal is not a giant AI platform. It is one clean output you can trust, with the source and audit trail attached.

My proof set covers three input types:

- Spreadsheets: cleanup, validation, deduplication, dashboards, and repeatable weekly reporting.
- PDF invoices: header fields, line items, math checks, exception logs, and Excel/CSV export.
- Meeting audio: ASR with timings, human correction, notes, decisions, risks, action items, and SRT.

I am based in Asia/Shanghai and work async. I do not require account passwords for the first sample or quote.
