## Gig description

If your meeting recording is full of useful decisions but nobody has time to turn it into notes, I can turn it into a clean summary, action list, and subtitle file.

Send one short sample recording and the output format you need. I will confirm fit, scope, and a fixed price before work starts.

**What I can produce**

- Meeting summary and key points
- Decisions and risks
- Action items with owner, task, and due date
- English transcript
- English SRT
- Chinese SRT
- Bilingual SRT
- Excel or CSV action list

**How I work**

1. Transcribe the recording with ASR and word timestamps.
2. Apply a human correction pass for names, numbers, and terms.
3. Extract decisions, risks, and action items.
4. Package notes and subtitle files.

The demo is transparent about raw ASR quality: it measures word error rate, then uses a human-corrected transcript for the final notes. I do not pretend the raw machine transcript is perfect.

A **72-second narrated walkthrough** is included with the case page: English voiceover, burned-in captions, real waveform, raw ASR errors, the human-correction step, action items, notes, and three SRT exports. The demo uses synthetic audio only.

**Good fit**

- Weekly team meetings
- Client calls and onboarding calls
- Interviews and coaching sessions
- Webinars and training recordings with clear audio
- English audio with English or Chinese subtitles

**Not a fit**

- Heavily overlapping speakers with no separate tracks
- Very noisy recordings or poor phone audio
- Legal, medical, or financial advice where a certified transcript is required
- Secret recording or content you do not have permission to process
- “100% accuracy” promises without a written quality spec

## FAQ

**Do you use AI?**  
Yes. ASR plus AI-assisted processing, with a human correction and review pass. I disclose it up front.

**Can you identify speakers?**  
Speaker labels are available when the recording has clear turns or separate tracks. Overlapping speakers may need a diarization upgrade.

**Can you translate into Chinese?**  
Yes. Standard and Premium include Chinese SRT. Other languages can be quoted separately.

**What if the audio is noisy?**  
Send a short sample first. I will tell you whether the quality is good enough or whether a cleanup/OCR-style pass is needed.

**Do you need access to my meeting account?**  
No. Send an exported audio file. Account access is not required for the quote or demo.

**Can you guarantee 100% accuracy?**  
No honest transcription workflow can guarantee that without a written quality spec. I use ASR, human correction, and a visible review step.

**Can you turn action items into a task list?**  
Yes. You get owner, task, due date, and source sentence in CSV/Excel.
