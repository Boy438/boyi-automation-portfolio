## Gig description

Need notes from a meeting recording — without pretending raw AI transcription is perfect?

I turn one recording into:

- a clean, readable summary
- key points, decisions, and risks
- action items with owner, task, due date, and the source sentence
- English, Chinese, or bilingual SRT (by package)
- a CSV/Excel action list (by package)

**How it works**

1. You send a 2–5 minute sample first.
2. I check audio quality, speaker turns, names, and output format.
3. I confirm fit, scope, and a fixed price before work starts.
4. I run ASR with timestamps, then do a human correction pass for names, numbers, and terms.
5. I extract actions/decisions from the corrected transcript — not from raw ASR.
6. I deliver notes, CSV/Excel, and SRT with a short review note for any uncertain names or dates.

**Before you order**

- Send a 2–5 minute sample first. If the sample does not fit, I will tell you before you order.
- Clear audio, one speaker at a time, or separate tracks for each speaker.
- A name list/glossary helps; without it, names are best-effort and flagged.
- This is **not** a certified legal/medical/financial transcript.
- I do **not** promise 100% accuracy.

**What you get**

- Reviewed notes (summary, key points, decisions, risks)
- Action items with owner, task, due date, and source sentence
- Clean, readable transcript (not certified verbatim)
- English / Chinese / bilingual SRT (by package)
- CSV or Excel action list (by package)
- A short review note for uncertain names/dates

**Good fit**

- Weekly team meetings, client calls, onboarding calls
- Interviews and coaching sessions
- Webinars/training with clear audio
- English audio with English or Chinese subtitles

**Not a fit**

- Heavily overlapping speakers with no separate tracks
- Very noisy recordings or phone-speaker recordings
- Legal, medical, or financial advice requiring a certified transcript
- Secret recording or content you do not have permission to process
- “100% accuracy” or unlimited revisions
- Account passwords or unattended access

## FAQ

**Is this a verbatim transcript?**
No. You get a clean, readable transcript with filler words removed. It is not a certified verbatim record.

**How accurate is it?**
ASR first, then a human correction pass. I do not promise 100% accuracy. Names, numbers, and dates depend on audio quality and the glossary you provide.

**Can you label speakers?**
Only when the recording has clear turns or separate tracks. Otherwise I use Speaker 1/2 or omit labels; I do not invent names.

**What audio do you accept?**
Clear exported audio (WAV/MP3/M4A). No phone-speaker recordings, heavy echo, or overlapping speakers without separate tracks.

**Do you need access to my meeting account?**
No. Send an exported audio file. I do not need account access.

**Can you guarantee 100% accuracy?**
No honest workflow can. I use ASR + human correction + a visible review note.

**What if the audio is noisy?**
Send a 2–5 minute sample first. I will tell you whether it is workable, needs cleanup, or is not a fit.

**Can you turn action items into a task list?**
Yes. You get owner, task, due date, due phrase, and source sentence in CSV/Excel.

**What if I need more revisions?**
The package includes a fixed number of revisions for the agreed scope. New requirements are a new order.
