| Python File & Folder Automation | $250 |

## Rules

- Do not launch at $5.
- Basic must be a complete small outcome, not a crippled trial.
- The middle package should be the best value; most buyers choose it.
- After 3 real reviews, raise prices 25%–30% across all gigs.
- Rush delivery: +$25–$30.
- Extra file / extra batch: quote after seeing the sample.
- No unlimited revisions.
- No free custom work before the order.

## Why this works

The buyer is not paying for “AI.” The buyer is paying for a clean, auditable output with a lower risk of manual rework. The price should reflect the QA, documentation, and exception handling, not just the generation speed.
