# Compliance Notes

## Platform rules

- No off-platform payment.
- No off-platform contact details in Fiverr gigs or messages.
- No “contact me on Telegram/WeChat/email” CTA inside Fiverr.
- No duplicate accounts.
- No fake reviews, fake client logos, or invented testimonials.
- No undisclosed fully automated delivery.

## Safe disclosure

Use this sentence:

> AI-assisted build, human-reviewed and tested. You receive the working files, source code where applicable, and an exception/test report.

## Accuracy language

Use:

> Controlled benchmark on synthetic demo data. Not a guarantee for every real-world file or recording.

Do not use:

- “100% accuracy”
- “perfect extraction”
- “fully automatic, no review needed”
- “works on every PDF/audio file”

## Data handling

- Start with masked or synthetic samples.
- Do not request account passwords.
- Do not accept sensitive data before scope and privacy are agreed.
- Do not process recordings without permission.
- Do not scrape sites in violation of their terms.

## Record keeping

- Save platform order records.
- Save withdrawal records and exchange rates.
- Keep a simple income ledger.
- Declare income according to local rules.
